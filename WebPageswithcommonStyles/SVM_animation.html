What to try

Kernel trick: load the "Circular" preset and train with a Linear kernel first — notice it can't separate the classes at all, since no straight line works. Switch to RBF and retrain: the boundary curves around the inner cluster. The RBF kernel implicitly maps points into a higher-dimensional space where a straight hyperplane can separate them — you never compute that mapping directly, only the kernel similarity $K(x_i, x_j)$.

C parameter: load "Overlapping," train with a small C (e.g. 0.2) and then a large C (e.g. 15). Small C allows more margin violations (a wider, softer margin, more tolerant of noise); large C penalizes violations heavily (a narrower margin that tries hard to classify every point correctly, risking overfitting to noisy points).

Support vectors: only the circled points influence the boundary — try dragging the idea further by clearing everything except the support vectors after training (the boundary should stay the same); every other point could move around freely without changing the hyperplane at all.
