MLP: The Translation Invariance Problem (Corrected)

(Visualization: Lack of weight sharing means location binds the learned weights.)

Strategic Insight: In an MLP, weights are location-bound. The weights ($w_0, w_4$) that learn a pattern at one spot are physically distinct from the weights ($w_4, w_8$) that must process the same pattern at a different spot. Since they are not shared, the pattern is perceived as completely different.
