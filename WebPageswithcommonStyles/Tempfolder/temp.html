The Backpropagation Pipeline (L → L-2)

Step 1: Layer $L$ (Output Layer)

The error is born here by comparing our guess to the ground truth.

$$dZ^{[L]} = \hat{y} - y$$
↓ Handover: Distribute Blame via Weights ↓
$$dA^{[L-1]} = W^{[L]T} \cdot dZ^{[L]}$$

Step 2: Layer $L-1$ (Hidden Layer)

The "Received Blame" hits the ReLU gate. If the neuron was inactive ($Z < 0$), the blame stops here.

$$dZ^{[L-1]} = dA^{[L-1]} \cdot f'(Z^{[L-1]})$$
↓ Handover: Chain continues to deeper layers ↓
$$dA^{[L-2]} = W^{[L-1]T} \cdot dZ^{[L-1]}$$
Architect's Note: Notice the pattern? To move blame back through a layer, we multiply by the Gate ($f'$). To move blame between layers, we multiply by the Weights ($W^T$).
